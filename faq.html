Frequently asked questions

The quick answers to keep you moving forward without waiting for support.

Does it work without Netflix Premium?

+

Yes. Any Netflix account tier works, including the ad-supported plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

+

Absolutely. Manage your subscription inside Settings → Subscription at any time.

Why no refunds during alpha?

+

Alpha pricing is deeply discounted and funds ongoing subtitle translations. You can cancel before renewal with no penalty.

Will other streaming sites be supported?

+

Yes! Prime Video and Disney+ are on the roadmap, and community votes help us choose the order.
