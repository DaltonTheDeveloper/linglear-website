How It Works

From signup to glowing fluency — your learning journey, clearly mapped.

1. Install Extension & Sign Up

Authenticate with Cognito, add our extension, and glide through setup.

2. Watch & Learn

Interactive subtitles track every phrase you love and save it to your word bank.

3. Vote for Next Translations

Cast glowing votes so the community can unlock new shows faster.

4. Earn Points by Helping Others

Submit corrections, host watch parties, and earn neon points.

Space your repetitions with flashcards and confetti-worthy quizzes.

6. Grow the Community

Refer friends, mentor newbies, and celebrate translation drops together.
