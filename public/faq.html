Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about the Alpha program, pricing, and how Linglear works with your favorite streaming services.

Does it work without Netflix Premium?

Linglear integrates with supported streaming accounts. Netflix Premium is recommended for simultaneous bilingual subtitles, but we provide alternative options for other tiers.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Manage your subscription directly in the Settings page and cancel with one click. You’ll keep access until the end of your billing cycle.

Why no refunds during alpha?

Alpha pricing is discounted heavily to help us cover infrastructure and translation costs. We focus on rapid iteration, but we’re always open to feedback if something isn’t right.

Will other streaming sites be supported?

Yes! Crunchyroll, Disney+, and Hulu are at the top of the list. Vote for your preferred integrations from the community dashboard.

Still need help?

Reach out via our contact form and we’ll respond within 24 hours.
