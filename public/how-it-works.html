How Linglear Works

From the extension install to community-powered translations, this is the transparent look at how we keep language learning fast, fun, and affordable.

1. Install Extension & Sign Up

Create your account, connect your favorite streaming services, and enable bilingual subtitles.

2. Watch & Learn

Enjoy immersive subtitles, vocabulary highlights, and study prompts directly in your shows.

3. Vote for Next Translations

Use your credits to prioritize shows and episodes the community is eager to translate.

4. Earn Points by Helping Others

Upload subtitles, proofread translations, and earn bonus points for tutoring sessions.

5. Practice with SRS

Send vocabulary to your spaced repetition deck and sync your progress with the mobile app.

6. Grow the Community

Invite friends, unlock achievements, and celebrate milestones together.

ML System Explained

We blend human-in-the-loop workflows with cost-efficient machine translation to deliver accurate subtitles. Every correction improves the model.

Credit Costs

Fund your own translation for quicker turnaround or donate credits to community votes.

Voting System

  1. Upload or nominate a subtitle track.
  2. Community votes and funds the translation.
  3. Progress notifications update everyone involved.
